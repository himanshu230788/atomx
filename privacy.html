AtomX Uploader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

AtomX Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the AtomX YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API and to read its YouTube Analytics.

It accesses only the AtomX channel owner's own YouTube data. It does not collect, sell, or share data about any other person, and it shares no data with third parties. OAuth tokens are stored privately and used only to manage the AtomX channel.

AtomX Uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at Google account permissions.
